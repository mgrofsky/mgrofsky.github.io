About

I am a security and technology executive with 30 years of experience building cybersecurity programs, software platforms, and engineering organizations. Today I serve as Chief Information Security Officer at Sharpen Technologies, where I lead security, platform engineering, CloudOps, and product management across Sharpen, Ytel, Plum Voice, and Webtext. Before the acquisition, I built and led multidisciplinary teams across engineering, SRE, and security at Ytel while architecting a large-scale cloud communications and developer platform.

My current work sits at the intersection of AI, security, and engineering operations. I use AI to run more work in parallel, but I do not give it unlimited authority or assume the result is correct. I define the scope, permissions, acceptance criteria, tests, and evidence required before calling the work complete. I hold a D.Eng. in Cybersecurity Analytics and an M.Eng. in Cybersecurity Policy and Compliance from The George Washington University, where I now teach as an adjunct professor and advise doctoral candidates in Cybersecurity Analytics.
